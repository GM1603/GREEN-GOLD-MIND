Our Story

From the Misty Hills of Kerala

Nestled in the lush green highlands of Kuttikkanam, Peermade — at an elevation of 3,500 feet in the Idukki district of Kerala — our tea gardens benefit from cool mountain air, rich soil, and pristine natural springs.

Green Gold Mind is born from a deep passion for authentic, artisanal tea-making. We use the traditional orthodox method to carefully roll intact leaves, preserving the delicate essential oils, amino acids, and natural antioxidants that give our tea its distinctive character.

Every sip carries the essence of our land — gentle floral notes, a clean natural sweetness, and a profound sense of well-being.

Preparation Guide

How to Brew the Perfect Cup

Follow these simple steps for an authentic whole-leaf green tea experience.

1

Measure

Place 3 grams of whole leaf green tea in a cup (~150ml capacity).

2

Heat Water

Use water below boiling point (70°–85°C) to prevent burning the delicate leaves.

3

Steep

Pour warm water over the leaves and let them steep for 5 minutes. Watch them unfurl beautifully.

4

Enjoy

Drink straight from the glass. The leaves can be infused a couple more times for additional cups.
